Video quality metrics saturate early

Detect early, intervene selectively

Waiting for a full video that misses the user's intent wastes both compute and time. We inspect early previews to catch likely failures, diagnose the mismatch, and intervene selectively. A lightweight single-frame probe guides semantic injection; visual feedback refines the prompt when needed. The aim is to reach a satisfactory video sooner, with less wasted generation.

Latent-to-RGB (L2R) module enables early failure detection with negligible resource overhead.

A tiny L2R module delivers previews ≈203× faster than the original CogVideoX decoder.

Future work

We aim to detect finer-grained failures earlier and develop more efficient, effective interventions. More expressive yet lightweight video evaluators could help identify subtle motion artifacts and compositional errors, reducing wasted generation compute for service providers and shortening the time to satisfactory videos for users.
